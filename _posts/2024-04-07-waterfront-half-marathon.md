I did the Auckland Waterfront Half Marathon [again](/2023/04/auckland-waterfront-half-marathon-2023.html) today. I didn't have as much time to train this year, partly because of the canoeing trip last week, and I ran it slightly differently --- starting at a slower pace and trying to make sure I could run the whole way. I'm pleased I achieved that. My final time was just slightly slower than last year --- [1:51:35](https://results.runningevents.co.nz/result/waterfront/2024/3010), with a nice steady pace throughout the whole run, so I'm reasonably happy with that.
