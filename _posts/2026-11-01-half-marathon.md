I did the Auckland half marathon again today. (I missed last year because I was away on a [Google trip](https://robert.ocallahan.org/2025/11/ai-road-trip.html). I did it in [1:56](https://www.sportsplits.com/races/auckland-marathon-2026/events/3/results/individuals/24238/), a few minutes faster than my time in [2024](https://robert.ocallahan.org/2024/11/half-marathon.html). I'm pretty pleased with the result --- I ran a consistent pace, I felt good the whole way, and I had not trained very hard in the lead-up. Lots of people commented positively on me running barefoot, which was nice. The weather was absolutely perfect, it's just a fantastic day in Auckland --- sunny, very light breeze, cool.

I'm getting older and I don't know how much longer I can keep cranking out these sub-2-hour halfs, but I want to keep doing it as long as I can!
